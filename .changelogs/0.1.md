* Modernize Enhanced Visuals for Forge 1.7.10 with the GTNH buildscript and UniMixins.
* Rewrite the effects to run once per client tick, with a GPU blur pipeline that leaves the depth buffer and projection alone.
* Make the mod safe on dedicated servers. Servers with the mod send exact damage types; clients still work without it.
* Add explosion deafness: dust, ear ringing and other sounds that fade back in, based on later Enhanced Visuals versions.
* Pick damage marks the way current Enhanced Visuals does, by weapon, attacker and damage type, from a damage marks file that is easy to extend for modded damage.
* Add drowning, which closes your vision in from the edges and fades sounds out.
* Muffle sounds through Sound Physics when it is installed, and lower their volume otherwise.
* Add rain droplets, hunger desaturation and optional persistent blood that washes off in water.
* Add per-effect settings, a Forge configuration screen with descriptions, and the `/evtest` command to preview effects and log hits.
